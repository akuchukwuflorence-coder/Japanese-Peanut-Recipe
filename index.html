Japanese Peanuts (Coated Groundnut)

Japanese Peanuts are groundnuts covered with a flour-based coating and then fried or baked.

They are crunchy, soft and nutty inside

They can be sweet, spicy or plain salty

Ingredients

  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup of raw groundnut
  • 1 cup of flour
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp H2O
  • 1 cap flavour of choice

Methods

  1. wash your raw groundnuts in salty water and sieve
  2. place under the sun to dry
  3. dissolve sugar in water and add flavour to it
  4. put your groundnut in a bowl
  5. add your sugar mixture to bowl of groundnut and shake
  6. repeat the previous 2 steps until your peanut is well coated
  7. fry in hot vegetable oil on a medium heat
  8. when they are brown in colour bring down to serve

Recipe Details

Preparation Time:15 minutes

Cook Time:20 minutes

Servings:4 people

Difficulties:Easy

Finished Dish

Tips

Do nut over crowd the oil while frying to ensure even cooking

Nutrition Facts

High in protein, health fats and energy
